What can you contact us about?

You might have a specific project in mind or a question you have not fully defined. These are examples of topics you can raise.

A business challenge

Explain where progress feels stuck, what you have tried and what you want to understand.

Customers and growth

Tell us about a customer, market, visibility, sales or follow-up question you want to investigate.

A website or business material

Describe the pages, presentation or other useful asset you want to build or improve.

AI or an existing system

Share the task, workflow or business process you would like to assess or review.

(02)

Send a short inquiry

Use the form below to introduce your question or project. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (*). The form is designed to gather only the details needed to begin.

Contact form

Please enter the following information. You can provide project documents or more detailed records later, if they are needed and an appropriate method of sharing has been agreed.

Your name, so we know how to address you.

An email address where you can receive a reply about your inquiry.

Your organization name or a relevant website link, if helpful.

Briefly describe the problem, project or question. Include the main goal and any important timing, if you know it.

We use the details you provide to review and respond to your inquiry. Please read our Privacy Policy before sending.

What happens after you reach out?

An inquiry is a starting point, not a project commitment. The next discussion should make the need, possible scope and practical arrangements clearer.

  1. 01

    We review what you have shared

    Your message provides a starting description of the business need. We may need to ask a follow-up question to understand the goal or check whether the request fits the services being discussed.

  2. 02

    We discuss a useful starting point

    If the request is suitable for further discussion, the next step may be to clarify the available information, intended audience, required outputs and any project constraints.

  3. 03

    We agree on a scope before work begins

    Where work is proposed, the project details should be confirmed before it starts: deliverables, roles, required inputs, review stages, timing, price and any implementation or support responsibilities.

What an initial message does not do

Sending an inquiry does not book a meeting, create an account, approve paid work or guarantee that a project can be accepted. Availability and a suitable next step are discussed as part of the response.

Need a better idea of the process?

See How We Work for the proposed stages from an initial discussion through scope, review and handover.

What is useful to include?

You can start with the one outcome that matters most. For example: what you want to understand, what you want to change, or what you need to have built. It is fine if the details are still developing.

(03)

Keep the first message simple

A short description is enough

A sentence about your organization, the main challenge and your intended next step can help us understand the request. A detailed project document is not required for an initial inquiry.

Protect private information

Please do not include passwords, payment details, government IDs, private customer records, medical information or other sensitive material in the form. If a project later requires restricted information, agree on a suitable sharing process first.

You can share files later, when appropriate

If an existing website, document or business record needs review, we can first clarify what is relevant, who is authorized to share it and what access or confidentiality arrangements are needed.

Contacting us from the United States or elsewhere

Businesses in the United States are a priority audience, and inquiries from other locations are welcome. Communication methods, meeting availability and working arrangements are discussed for a defined project.

(04)

Questions about getting in touch

Do I need to choose a service before writing?

No. Tell us what you need to understand, improve or build. You can explore services first, but the inquiry does not depend on choosing one.

Can I ask about one website or business document?

Yes. A website, landing page, brochure, presentation or other defined asset can be discussed as a standalone project.

Can businesses outside the United States get in touch?

Yes. The United States is a priority market, and inquiries from other countries are welcome. Project arrangements depend on the actual delivery requirements.

Is the first conversation free?

The initial inquiry does not create a paid project. Any consultation scope, assessment fee or other commercial arrangement must be clarified before you agree to work.

Should I attach private files or customer information?

No. The initial form does not require file uploads or sensitive records. Discuss any document or access needs separately before sharing them.

How quickly will I receive a reply?

You can mention a deadline in your message. Any reply arrangements and available next steps can be discussed after your inquiry has been reviewed.

Ready to introduce your project?

Tell us the business question or material you need help with. Keep it short and useful; detailed scope and next steps can be discussed after the inquiry.
