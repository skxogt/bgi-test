About BGI Solutions

Business questions deserve clear, useful answers.

When you run a business, it can be hard to know whether to investigate a problem, change a process or build something new. You need to understand the options before committing time and money.

BGI Solutions brings business assessment and growth planning together with website and business-material production. The idea is to make each project easier to explain: what needs attention, what work is proposed and what you can expect to receive.

Talk to UsExplore Services

What BGI Solutions is here to help with

You may need to understand what is slowing progress, learn more about your customers, make your offer easier to find or explain, or produce a website or useful business material. These are different needs, and they do not all require the same kind of work.

BGI Solutions organizes its documented service areas so you can start with a business question or a defined project. You do not have to buy a large program before discussing a single useful piece of work.

For business owners and decision-makers

We explain our services for the people making decisions about their business, not only specialists who already know the terminology. Our aim is to use plain English, show what a project can produce and be clear about what is included.

We welcome inquiries from businesses in the United States and other markets. Practical communication and delivery arrangements are agreed for each project, rather than assumed from geography.

Five connected areas of business support

The BGI Solutions portfolio is organized into five areas. Each contains services with a defined focus; the right starting point depends on your question, available information and the work you want done.

  • A

    Diagnose, Prioritize & Strategize

    Understand your business and plan growth

    Investigate growth problems, compare opportunities, prioritize decisions and develop an actionable plan based on the information available.

  • B

    Market, Customer & Competitive Intelligence

    Know your customers and market

    Study customer needs, competitors, market choices and external changes so your decisions can be better informed.

  • C

    Visibility, Trust, Demand & Acquisition

    Help customers find and choose your business

    Review discoverability, trust, useful content, lead handling, buying decisions and the cost of reaching new customers.

  • D

    Customer Value, Retention & Asset Production

    Improve customer value and build useful materials

    Plan clearer onboarding and customer follow-up, or create the website, landing page, presentation or other material defined in a project brief.

  • E

    AI, Optimization & Continuous Intelligence

    Use AI and keep improving

    Assess whether a specific task is suitable for AI, or define a careful review process for growth systems that are already in use.

A defined project can stand on its own

These areas are connected, but they are not one compulsory package. You can discuss one assessment, one website or one piece of content. If a later step requires implementation, ongoing management or additional research, it receives its own agreed scope.

The thinking behind BGI Solutions

BGI Solutions is being developed around a straightforward principle: a business service should make the next decision easier to understand. Clear questions, useful evidence and defined outputs matter more than complicated service labels.

The plan is to connect insight with practical work when appropriate. Some projects can end with a clear assessment or roadmap. Others call for a website, sales material or an agreed improvement to an existing process. Each should have its own purpose, boundaries and review points.

What BGIECO™ means here

BGIECO™ is the framework used to organize the documented 20-service portfolio. It connects the five service areas under one understandable structure, helping you explore a relevant need without presenting every service as a required bundle.

The framework is a way to navigate the service areas, not a required program. You can begin with one defined question or project without committing to the full portfolio.

Principles behind the proposed work

Start with the real question

Understand what you need to decide, improve or build before choosing activities or tools.

Explain what is known

Distinguish supplied facts, working assumptions and information that still needs checking.

Make the output tangible

Agree whether you need findings, a plan, written materials, a website or an explicitly scoped implementation.

Give people a clear review role

Identify who provides information, approves content and decides what happens after handover.

Be clear about limits

Business outcomes depend on the client, the market, the implementation and the evidence available; they cannot be promised by a written plan alone.

The people behind the business

Good business work depends on people who understand the brief, take responsibility for their part and communicate clearly. For an agreed project, the roles, review contacts and approval responsibilities should be made clear before work begins.

What working together can look like

A conversation starts with the business need. We then identify the information to review, discuss the project boundary and agree what will be produced. The written agreement defines responsibilities, feedback points, timing, price and any follow-up or implementation included.

For website or asset projects, we also identify the actual content sources, approval owner, delivery files and relevant checks. For assessment work, we define the question, evidence available and limitations of the findings.

Working with clients in different locations

Businesses in the United States are a priority audience. When you contact us, we can discuss the time zones, meeting options and communication arrangements that would suit a proposed project. We agree the practical details before work starts.

See examples that explain the work

Useful work examples explain the problem, the agreed scope and what was produced. Website demonstrations are clearly labeled, while real client examples require permission. We show the work itself rather than imply a result the evidence cannot support.

Questions about BGI Solutions

What does BGI Solutions do?

The documented service portfolio covers business assessment, customer and market research, growth planning, online visibility and conversion topics, customer experience, websites, business materials and selected AI or ongoing-improvement work. Each project needs its own agreed scope.

Do I need to choose one of the 20 services first?

No. Describe what you want to understand, improve or create. We can discuss which service area may fit the need and what a manageable starting project could include.

What is BGIECO™?

It is the framework used to organize the BGI Solutions service portfolio into five connected areas. It helps explain the work; it is not a guarantee of results.

Do you work with businesses in the United States?

US businesses are a priority audience, and we welcome inquiries from other markets too. Available communication, meeting times, delivery arrangements and any location-specific considerations are agreed before a project.

How can I see examples of the work?

Where available, we identify real, permissioned project examples and clearly labeled demonstrations. We explain what was produced without presenting a demonstration as a completed client engagement.

Who will handle my project, and how is pricing agreed?

Before work begins, the project agreement should make clear who will be your contact, who approves the work, what you will receive, the review points, the price and schedule, and any third-party costs.

Find out how a project could begin

You can read the working process or explore the service areas first. If you already know the problem you want to solve or the material you need to build, tell us about it and the information you have.
