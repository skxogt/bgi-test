(04)

What working with us looks like

  1. 01

    Start with your need

    Tell us what you want to understand, improve or build. Share what is happening now and what you have already tried. You do not need to choose a service before contacting us.

  2. 02

    Agree on the work

    We define the deliverables, responsibilities, price and schedule before work starts. Your proposal explains what is included, what you need to provide and when you will review the work.

  3. 03

    Create and review

    We review the work with you at agreed stages. Your feedback helps confirm that the content, recommendations or finished materials address the brief.

  4. 04

    Check the outputs and decide what comes next

    We check the completed work against the agreed requirements and explain the handover. Any publication, implementation or ongoing support is included only when it is part of the project scope.

(05)

Your questions answered

Do I need to choose a service before contacting you?

You can describe the problem or project in your own words. We can discuss whether a service fits and what information would help clarify the work.

Can I start with a website or business materials?

Yes. Tell us what you need, who will use it and what you already have. We can review a defined production project directly. A business assessment is a separate service when the underlying problem needs investigation.

What will I receive from a project?

Your proposal lists the deliverables and review steps. Depending on the scope, you may receive assessment findings, research, a growth roadmap, website pages or business materials. The proposal also explains delivery formats and any remaining setup.

How are pricing and timing decided?

They depend on the work required, the information available and the review arrangements. We discuss the scope, price and expected schedule before you decide to proceed.

Who owns the finished work?

Ownership and usage rights are set out in the project agreement. Third-party images, software and other licensed materials may have separate terms. We identify those terms as part of the agreed handover.

How will we know whether the work has helped?

We agree how to review the completed work before starting. If the scope includes tracking business results, we define the measures, information and responsibilities needed to evaluate them after the work is put into use.

Tell us what you need help with

What would you like to understand, improve or build? Share a short description of your business and the problem or project. We can discuss a useful starting point and what information is needed next.
