SVC-12CVisibility, Trust, Demand & Acquisition

Reach people who are a better fit for your business.

More inquiries are not always better inquiries. BGI Solution helps you define the customers you want to reach, explain your offer clearly and plan a useful path from initial interest to the next conversation.

We begin with whom you want to serve and what makes an inquiry relevant. Then we consider suitable messages, channels, inquiry questions and follow-up responsibilities. The agreed project sets out what will be planned, built or managed.

Talk to UsExplore All Services

(01)

When this service may help

You receive inquiries that rarely fit

People contact you, but their needs, location, timing or budget may not match the work you can take on.

Your audience and message are too broad

Potential customers may not recognize whether your offer is meant for them or what they should do next.

Interested people cannot take the next step easily

Forms, contact paths or handoffs may be confusing or may not collect the information needed for a useful reply.

Follow-up is inconsistent

Your team may not have clear criteria for assigning inquiries, responding, or identifying which channels attract appropriate interest.

(02)

Start with the customers you want to reach

Tell us what you sell, who it best serves and what a useful inquiry looks like. We can discuss a focused demand plan, an inquiry workflow, or a separately agreed implementation project.

This service is about attracting and handling relevant interest. Sales conversations, closed deals, advertising spend and ongoing campaign operations are separate responsibilities unless explicitly included.

(03)

How a demand generation project can work

The work starts with a clear definition of fit. These four stages illustrate a possible approach; the project proposal confirms activities, access, deliverables and review points.

  1. 01

    Define your audience and an inquiry that fits

    Identify the customer groups, needs and situations the business can serve. Agree practical qualification criteria, such as service need, service area, expected timing and other appropriate information. Avoid collecting details that are not necessary for the inquiry.

  2. 02

    Choose relevant messages and channels

    Review what the audience needs to understand before getting in touch. Consider suitable channels, such as existing website pages, useful content, referrals or separately scoped campaigns. Compare options against available evidence, costs, capacity and permissions rather than assuming every channel is right.

  3. 03

    Plan capture, qualification and handover

    Define a simple next step and the minimum useful inquiry fields. Specify how information is recorded, how fit is assessed, who receives each inquiry and what happens when an inquiry is not suitable. Include access controls, privacy notices and any applicable consent requirements in the agreed implementation brief.

  4. 04

    Agree measures and review responsibilities

    Choose meaningful measures such as suitable inquiries, source information when available, handover completeness and follow-up progress. Set the starting information, reporting period, data limitations and who will review or improve the process.

(04)

The goal is useful demand, not a promised lead count

A workable demand system connects the intended audience, clear messages, a relevant invitation and an accountable handover. Performance depends on market conditions, the offer, resources, channels and follow-up; no inquiry or sales volume can be guaranteed.

(05)

What you can receive

Two proposed outputs can make the work easier to plan and assess. Your agreement confirms whether you need recommendations, a defined setup or ongoing management.

Demand Generation Plan

A practical plan that defines the intended audience, the problem the offer addresses and the channels and messages to consider. It describes what makes an inquiry relevant, what action a prospect should take, and the main assumptions to test.

The plan may identify channel priorities, content or landing-page needs, required approvals, data and measurement sources, review dates and constraints. It states whether campaign setup, creative production, tools and paid media are included or only recommended.

Inquiry and Handover Workflow

A clear route from an initial question to the appropriate follow-up. It can specify inquiry fields, qualification questions, fit criteria, routing rules, notification responsibilities and how incomplete or unsuitable inquiries are handled.

The workflow can also define response ownership, recordkeeping, privacy and access controls, escalation points and useful handover measures. The brief confirms whether any forms, CRM changes, automations or integrations will actually be configured and tested.

(06)

Implementation, privacy and measurement boundaries

Your proposal states what will be researched, planned, written, configured, launched or managed. Advertising budgets, third-party subscriptions, outreach, platform costs, contact-list use, data retention and ongoing optimization require explicit agreement and appropriate authorization.

Measures reflect available records and an agreed review period. Attribution may be incomplete, and a relevant inquiry is not the same as a sale. We do not guarantee lead counts, conversions, revenue, cost reductions or campaign return.

(07)

A sample project

Sample project

A service business receives many inquiries outside its usual work

Imagine a service company that handles a defined range of projects but receives messages for jobs it cannot support. Its website describes the service broadly, and the contact form does not help visitors understand whether the company is the right fit.

A focused project could clarify the audience and service boundaries, compare relevant ways to reach those customers, and propose more useful website language and inquiry questions. The team could define how suitable requests are passed to the person responsible for replying.

A Demand Generation Plan might guide the next experiment, while an Inquiry and Handover Workflow explains how messages are assessed and routed. This example does not claim that a campaign launched, leads increased or sales improved.

(08)

What we may need from you

Share your services, intended customers, available sales capacity, existing website or channels and any suitable inquiry records. We discuss what information is genuinely necessary, what can be shared safely and who can authorize access before reviewing customer data.

(09)

What we agree before starting

Audience and fit

Define the customer, qualifying conditions, exclusions, primary offer and appropriate next step.

Channels and scope

Agree research, messages, content, landing pages, forms, campaign tools and any media or external spending included.

Data and handover

Confirm lawful collection and use, notices or consent where needed, access, qualification rules, owner of follow-up and any technical integrations.

Measures and commercial terms

Establish initial records, review period, data limitations, deliverables, approval steps, price, expected schedule and ongoing responsibilities.

(10)

Questions about lead generation and demand

Do we need advertising to get started?

No. We can discuss a defined planning project first. Possible channels depend on your audience, offer and available resources. Paid campaigns are included only when specifically agreed.

What makes an inquiry qualified?

It is an inquiry that meets criteria agreed with your business. The criteria may address the relevant need, service location, timing or other sensible requirements. Meeting those criteria does not guarantee a sale.

Can you improve our existing contact form and handover?

We can review an existing form and workflow and propose changes. Building forms, connecting systems, testing routing and maintaining integrations are included only if those technical tasks appear in the project scope.

Will you run campaigns and follow up with every lead?

The proposal specifies any campaign delivery, platform management and follow-up work. Your business or another approved provider may handle replies and sales conversations; those duties must be assigned explicitly.

How will we know if the work is helping?

We first confirm that the agreed plan or workflow meets the brief. Where outcomes are in scope, we compare available baseline records and agreed measures over a stated period, while noting missing source data and other influences.

Are leads or new customers guaranteed?

No. Inquiry fit and volume depend on the offer, audience, channels, competition and follow-up. We can agree a sensible measurement approach, but not promise a particular number of leads, conversions or customers.
