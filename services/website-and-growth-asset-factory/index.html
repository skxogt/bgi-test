SVC-18DCustomer Value, Retention & Asset Production

Build a website that makes your services easy to understand.

Your website and business materials should help people understand what you offer, who it is for and what to do next. Clear words, useful design and dependable delivery all need to work together.

BGI Solutions plans and produces websites, landing pages, sales materials and useful business resources. We connect content, design and production through a defined brief, so everyone can review what the project is meant to deliver.

(01)

When this service may help

Your website is difficult to follow

Visitors may struggle to find important services or understand what makes an offer relevant to them.

You need a focused landing page

A new offer, event or campaign may need clear messaging and an agreed next action.

Your sales team needs consistent materials

A brochure, presentation or proposal may need approved explanations and a professional design.

Your existing materials need improvement

Outdated pages or files may need revised wording, layout, features or handover formats.

(02)

Start with the asset you need

Tell us what you want to produce, who will use it and what you already have. We can discuss a defined website or materials project directly; a separate assessment is not required.

Each proposal specifies the pages, assets, features, review stages and responsibilities included. Hosting, launch, integrations, ongoing marketing and support are included only when agreed.

(03)

How a website or asset project takes shape

A practical project starts with its purpose, not a template. The work can follow four stages, with the agreed brief setting the deliverables, approvals and technical boundaries.

  1. 01

    Agree the brief

    Identify the intended audience, what they need to understand and the action the finished material should support. Confirm the pages or assets, available business facts, features, formats, reviewers and delivery platform.

  2. 02

    Plan the content and design

    Organize clear messages and an appropriate page or document structure. Review proposed layouts, accessible reading order, mobile behavior and any interactions before moving into final production.

  3. 03

    Produce and review

    Create the agreed website pages or materials. Review them with the business for factual accuracy, usable design, specified features, required permissions and feedback at the planned stages.

  4. 04

    Check and hand over

    Check the work against the brief and deliver the agreed files or accessible handover materials. Record completed checks, unresolved issues and any setup or publication actions still assigned to the client or another provider.

(04)

Know what the handover includes

The project agreement defines what is built, which files are supplied, who approves them and what testing is included. A production handover is not a promise that a site has launched or achieved results.

(05)

Choose the materials your business needs

The following are possible packages or outputs, not a fixed bundle. Your project brief selects the items, features, formats and revisions that apply.

01

Website Growth Package

Agreed website pages and supporting files shaped around your audience and purpose. A brief may include page structure, approved copy, responsive layouts and specified functionality.

Define the number of pages, platform, mobile needs, accessibility expectations, assets, and any forms or integrations before production. Content approval and testing responsibilities are recorded.

02

Conversion Asset Package

A focused landing page or related material that explains an offer and provides a clear next step, such as asking a question or requesting a quote.

The brief defines the offer, message, action, form fields and any connection to another system. Lead volume and conversion results are not guaranteed.

03

Sales Asset Package

A brochure, presentation, proposal document or other agreed material that helps explain your services during a sales conversation, using approved facts and supported claims.

04

Authority Asset Package

An agreed guide or reference resource created from approved expertise and sources. Producing the asset is distinct from planning or running a wider content publishing program.

(06)

Prepare the files for real use

Your selected assets should have a clear destination: a website platform, a sales conversation, a customer download or another agreed channel.

Deployment-Ready Growth Assets

Final files and instructions prepared for the agreed platform or use, together with a record of completed checks and remaining setup. Publication, hosting, domain purchases and support require explicit agreement.

What belongs in the project agreement

Confirm pages or materials, approved content, required features, formats, revisions, review rounds, expected schedule, price, ownership and third-party licenses. Name who handles integrations, hosting, publication, maintenance and unresolved work.

Review the work before use

Website checks may cover wording, navigation, mobile layouts, forms, links, and agreed accessibility or performance requirements. Materials checks may cover readability, accuracy, layout and file usability. Test results belong to the implemented project, not this specification.

(07)

A sample project

Sample project

A service company needs a clearer website and proposal deck

Imagine a business whose website lists services in dense paragraphs while its proposal slides use outdated language. A defined project could organize customer questions, revise the service pages and create an approved presentation that follows the same explanation.

A Website Growth Package could address the pages; a Sales Asset Package could cover the deck. The handover would record actual files and any remaining platform setup, with separate approval for launch and support.

(08)

Questions about websites and business materials

Can we start with a website or materials project?

Yes. Share what you need, who will use it and the purpose it should serve. Wider research or growth strategy is separate work if needed.

Can you improve an existing website or file?

Share the current material and desired changes. The review confirms what can be reused and whether the platform, source files and rights support the requested work.

Will you host or publish the finished website?

Only when those activities are included in the agreement. Domains, hosting, platform subscriptions, launch and ongoing technical support are separately assigned.

What files and features are included?

That depends on the brief. It specifies the pages or assets, file formats, responsive layouts, forms, integrations, testing and handover instructions being delivered.

Who owns the completed work?

The agreement defines ownership and usage rights for the commissioned materials and any third-party images, fonts, software or other licenses.

Can you guarantee more visitors or inquiries?

No. Clear content and useful design may help people understand and act, but traffic and inquiries depend on many factors. Search visibility and ongoing optimization are separate scopes.
