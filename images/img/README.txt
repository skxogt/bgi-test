IMAGES AND VIDEO FOR THE BGI SOLUTION WEBSITE
=============================================

All images and the Home page video live in THIS folder: images/img/

Photos here are web-sized JPEG copies (about 1000 px wide for cards and the
gallery, 1800 px for wide images). That keeps pages fast: the full-size files
made the Our Work page 45 MB and caused stutter when scrolling.
Your full-size originals are untouched in images/img/originals/.

To replace a photo: delete the old img_XX file here, then add yours with the
same name (.jpg .jpeg .png .webp all work). Keep it around 1000-1800 px wide
and under ~400 KB if you can.
Diagrams: the .svg files below are shown whole, never cropped.
